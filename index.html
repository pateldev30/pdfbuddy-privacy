PDFBuddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

PDFBuddy ("the app", "we") is a free Android app for working with PDF files: merging, compressing, splitting, turning photos into PDFs and saving PDF pages as images. This policy explains what happens to your files and data when you use it.

The short version

  • Your PDF files are processed on your phone. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • PDFBuddy has no account and no sign-in, and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Your files

When you choose PDFs or photos with Android's file picker, PDFBuddy gets permission to read only the files you selected. It makes a temporary private copy on your phone to work on, and it never changes your original files.

Results (PDFs and images) are saved in the app's private storage until you save or share them. You decide where they go: Save to device, Open in another app, or Share.

Temporary copies are deleted when you leave a tool. Private result copies are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

We do not read, store, analyse or transmit the contents of your documents. Passwords you enter for protected PDFs are used only on your phone to open that file and are never stored.

PDFBuddy needs no storage permission. It cannot see any file you did not pick.

Data stored on your phone

PDFBuddy stores one small setting on your phone: your theme choice (light, dark or system). If you use Android's backup, only this setting may be included in your Google account backup. Your PDFs and results are never included in backups.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

PDFBuddy is free because it shows ads on the home screen and on result screens, never while your files are being processed. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • information about ad interactions and app usage, such as ads viewed and tapped
  • diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data

This data is collected and handled by Google, not by us. Learn more in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or another region where the law requires it, PDFBuddy asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings: Settings → Google → Ads (wording may vary by device).
  • PDFBuddy itself contains no other analytics or tracking tools.

Network use

PDFBuddy connects to the internet only to load ads and the consent form from Google. All PDF tools work fully offline.

Children

PDFBuddy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, there is nothing about you stored on our side to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the choices above or Google's tools at https://myaccount.google.com. Uninstalling PDFBuddy removes all of its data from your phone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version in the app and at the same web address.
